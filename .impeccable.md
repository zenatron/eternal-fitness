## Design Context

### Users
Small group of friends and family tracking fitness together. Personal but social — the app should feel like it belongs to a tight crew, not a corporate product. Used during workouts, post-workout logging, and checking progress throughout the day.

### Brand Personality
**Energetic. Kinetic. Communal.**
High-energy, vibrant, dynamic — the app should feel alive and motivating. It's the feeling of walking into a gym with great music playing. Not corporate, not clinical. This is YOUR tool and it should feel powerful in your hands.

### Aesthetic Direction — "FORGE"
- **Theme**: Dark mode primary, light mode secondary
- **Concept**: Molten energy — the app feels like a furnace of stored kinetic energy
- **Color palette**: Deep warm charcoals (`surface-*`) with vivid amber/orange accents (`forge-*`), coral/red for destructive actions (`ember-*`)
- **Typography**: Oswald (display, condensed, uppercase, athletic) + Manrope (body, geometric humanist sans)
- **Background**: Canvas ember particle system — slow-rising particles with warm orange glow
- **Interactions**: Warm "heat glow" on hover (cards emit forge-orange ambient light in dark mode), spring physics
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards, sterile medical apps, corporate fitness platforms, cyan/purple AI palettes

### Design Principles
1. **Energy in motion** — Every interaction should feel alive. Static is dead. The app breathes, responds, and rewards interaction.
2. **Bold, not loud** — High contrast and vivid color, but with restraint. Impact comes from a few strong moments, not everything screaming.
3. **Feels personal** — This isn't a product for millions. It should feel handcrafted, opinionated, like it was built by someone who lifts.
4. **Dark-first presence** — Dark backgrounds are the canvas. Color is the energy on top. Light mode adapts the energy, doesn't dilute it.
5. **Motion with purpose** — Animations communicate state, celebrate achievement, and create rhythm. Never decorative for decoration's sake.

### Design Tokens
- **Display font**: `font-display` → Oswald (condensed, uppercase for headings)
- **Body font**: `font-body` → Manrope (geometric, warm)
- **Primary accent**: `forge-500` (#ed7b16) — vivid orange-amber
- **Card style**: `forge-card` class (warm charcoal, subtle border, heat-glow on hover)
- **Backgrounds**: `surface-0` (near-black) through `surface-950` (warm cream)
- **Gradients**: `greeting-gradient` (slow-burning amber/coral oklch gradient)
